# Morphix Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 25 November 2026_

Morphix (published in the Microsoft Store as **Morphix Studio**) is a file toolkit that runs on your own computer. This policy explains what happens to your data.

## Your files stay on your computer

- Every conversion, compression, OCR, signature, scan and edit is done locally on your device.
- Morphix never uploads your files, their contents or their names to any server.
- New files are saved next to your originals (or in the folder you choose). Your originals are not changed.

## No accounts, no tracking

- Morphix does not ask you to create an account or sign in.
- It does not collect analytics, usage statistics, crash reports, advertising identifiers or any personal information.
- It does not show ads.

## When Morphix uses the network

Morphix works offline. It only uses the network in these cases, and only when you ask for them:

1. **Optional add-ons.** Some features need a model file the first time you use them (speech-to-text, improved OCR, background removal). When you choose to download one, Morphix downloads that file from its public source (Hugging Face, GitHub or ModelScope). Only a normal download request is sent; no personal data or files are sent. After that the feature works offline.
2. **Send from your phone.** This feature starts a small server on your own local network (Wi-Fi) so your phone can send photos, videos and files to your computer. It is reachable only on your local network, only while the window is open, and only with the one-time link or QR code shown on your screen. Nothing goes through the internet or any outside server.
3. **Links you open.** Buttons such as "GitHub" or "Download LibreOffice" open your web browser. What happens on those websites is covered by their own policies.

## Data stored on your computer

Morphix keeps your settings (language, shortcut, hub layout, recent choices) and optional saved signatures on your computer only. In the Microsoft Store version, uninstalling Morphix removes them.

## Children

Morphix does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.
